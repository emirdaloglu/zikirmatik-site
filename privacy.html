Privacy Policy

In short

Zikir+ collects no personal data. The app never connects to the internet. Your dhikrs, counts, statistics and settings are stored only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Data we collect

None. The app contains none of the following:

  • Accounts, sign-up, login or email collection
  • Advertising or ad networks
  • Analytics, usage measurement or crash reporting
  • Tracking cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or fingerprinting
  • Servers, databases or cloud backups
  • Access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos

Where your data lives

Your dhikrs and statistics are kept in your device's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them; there is no other copy. If you have iCloud backup enabled, they may be included in Apple's own device backup — that is entirely between you and Apple, and we have no access to it.

Notifications

Friday reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No remote (push) notifications are used: there is no server, no device push token is created, and nothing is registered anywhere. You can turn reminders off in Settings.

Home screen widgets

The widgets show your active dhikr and a summary of your statistics. They read that information within the same device through Apple's App Group storage. No data leaves the device.

Rating prompt

If you choose to rate the app, Apple's own rating window opens. Apple receives the rating and review; we never see who wrote what and receive no information.

Third parties

The app sends no data to any third-party service. The software libraries it uses (Apple's own frameworks and open-source interface libraries) run inside the app and do not talk to the outside. Fonts are embedded in the app and are not downloaded at runtime.

Deleting your data

  • To clear statistics: Settings → Reset Statistics
  • To delete one dhikr: Dhikrs → Edit → delete
  • To delete everything: remove the app from your device

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either. There is no separate handling for users under 13, as there is no data being processed.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised. Should anything material change — such as beginning to collect data — it will also be announced inside the app.
